# VLM fast path — 2026-11-19

The fix is in commits 09d9a1a and 0cb706e (`d2qual/vlm_ft.py`). The check is `scripts/vlm_speed.py`, run on a Runpod Secure Cloud A40 (pod `wqbhhbivrxe4na`, deleted afterwards).

## Why it was slow

In the COCO run, the 12-member ensemble managed only 256 of 300 images in 50 minutes, about 11.7 s per image. There were three causes:

1. **Batched vision attention.** In transformers 4.51, Qwen2-VL's "sdpa" vision attention builds one full (P×P) mask over the patches of every image in the call. The work therefore grew with the square of the batch size. A batch of 16 photos × 4 questions came to 65k patches.
2. **Repeated vision passes.** Each photo went through the vision tower 4 times (once per limb question) and again for every adapter and every mirror view: 24 vision passes per photo.
3. **Full-vocabulary logits.** Qwen2-VL in 4.51 has no `logits_to_keep`, so logits over the whole 151k-token vocabulary were computed at every position, about 6 GB per batch.

**The fix:**
- The vision tower runs once per photo, with one photo per call.
- Those image embeddings are reused for all 4 questions and for every adapter.
- Only the 4 answer-letter logits are computed, at the last position.

The same code serves training, so training got faster too.

## Results (A40, 300 images, adapters freshly initialised; speed doesn't depend on the trained values)

| Setting | s / image | projected for 2,000 images | peak GPU memory |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 adapters + mirror TTA (the default) | 0.757 | 25.2 min | 7.8 GB |
| 3 adapters, no TTA (`D2_VLM_TTA=0`) | 0.381 | 12.7 min | 7.8 GB |
| 1 adapter + mirror TTA | 0.326 | 10.9 min | 7.6 GB |

- **Inference:** before, about 11.7 s per image for the whole ensemble. That figure includes the CNN members, but the VLM was the bulk of it. The VLM alone is now about 15× faster.
- **Same answers:** the fast and original paths picked the same answer for every image and limb (argmax agreement 1.00). The largest probability difference was 0.030, which is bf16 rounding.
- **Training:** one LoRA step at batch 4 takes 1.06 s, versus about 3.7 s before (511 s per epoch over 137 steps). Peak memory is 24.5 GB. Training runs on our own pod, so the 16 GB limit doesn't apply to it.

## What this means for the submission

- The full COCO-picked ensemble now fits the time limit. Projected for 2,000 images:
  - VLM, 3 adapters + TTA: about 25 min
  - CNN/ViT folds: under 6.4 min each, from the earlier fit checks
  - pose: about 5.5 min at 164 ms per image
- That is roughly 40 min against the 50-minute budget in `predict.py`, which is too tight.
- So start with `D2_VLM_TTA=0` or a single adapter, then re-time the final container on the real test-set size.
- Peak inference memory is well under the 16 GB GPU limit.
